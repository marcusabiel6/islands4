how to play

islands is a two-player territory game on a 6x6 board. red goes first.

  • each player takes turns clicking empty squares.
  • you can only claim squares that are adjacent to your existing islands.
  • the goal is to have more islands than your opponent when no more moves are possible.
  • the game ends automatically when no valid moves are left for either player.
  • the player with fewer islands loses; equal number of islands results in a tie.
  • strategically expand and block your opponent to win!
